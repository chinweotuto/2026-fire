Pre-Match Feature Engineering & Explainability

Features are computed strictly prior to match kickoff. No post-match or future information is permitted.

⚽ Football Features

  • Team Elo: Dynamic ratings with +65 Home advantage
  • Form Windows: Last 3, 5, 10 matches (points, goals for/against)
  • Home/Away Splits: Home team home form & away team away form
  • Attack & Defense: Shots, shots on target, corners, goals per game
  • Schedule Context: Rest days, matches in last 7 and 14 days

🎾 Tennis Features

  • Player Elo: Overall Elo + Surface Elo (Hard, Clay, Grass)
  • Surface Form: Surface win rate over last 5 & 10 matches
  • Serve & Return: 1st serve in %, 1st/2nd serve won, break points
  • Fatigue Index: Recent match duration & rest days
  • Head-to-Head: Strict pre-match historical H2H record

🏀 Basketball Features

  • Team Elo: Dynamic ratings with +80 Home court advantage
  • Offensive Rating: Estimated ORtg, eFG%, 3P%, FT%
  • Defensive Rating: DRtg and opponent points allowed
  • Pace & Possession: Estimated pace, assist rate, turnover rate
  • Fatigue: Back-to-back indicator & rest days

⚠️ Statistical Attribution Note

Feature attributions represent predictive associations derived from historical data. They do not imply physical causation. Feature contributions are normalized and time-stamped.
