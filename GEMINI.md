# Antigravity Prediction Protocol & Standing Rules

Whenever sports matches or fixture lists (Football, Basketball, Tennis) are provided:

1. **Mandatory Deep Statistical Investigation**:
   - For every team/participant, calculate recent scoring and conceding rates (PPG / Goals), isolated home vs. away splits, direct Head-to-Head (H2H) results, and current momentum.
   - For Basketball: Analyze Pace (possessions per game), Offensive Rating (ORtg), Defensive Rating (DRtg), and 4-factors.
   - For Football: Analyze Expected Goals (xG), clean sheet rates, field tilt, and Dixon-Coles expected joint goal distribution.

2. **Market Priority (Over / Under Totals Focus)**:
   - Primary markets: **Game Over**, **Game Under**, **Team Over**, **Team Under**, and period-specific splits (**1st Half / 1st Quarter Over / Under**).

3. **Gating & Format**:
   - Prioritize statistical confidence and edge over raw quantity.
   - Output structured tables with:
     - Match & Tournament
     - Market & Line
     - Projected Total
     - Confidence Level (%)
     - Concrete statistical reason (H2H, PPG, defensive efficiency)
   - Include categorized accumulator / parlay slip suggestions (Banker Safe, High-Pace Overs, Defensive Unders).
