# Match Selection & Predictive Modeling Standing Protocol

Every time matches/fixtures are provided for prediction and analysis, strictly execute the following end-to-end evaluation process without needing further reminders:

---

## 1. Deep Pre-Match Statistical Analysis
Before making any pick, perform an exhaustive statistical breakdown for every fixture:
- **Scoring & Conceding Records**: Analyze exact goals/points scored and allowed over the last 5, 10, and 20 matches.
- **Home vs. Away Splits**: Isolated venue analysis (home team's true home performance vs. away team's true away performance).
- **Head-to-Head (H2H) Historical Data**: Direct encounters, recent margin trends, and stylistic match-ups.
- **Form & Pace Momentum**: Track exponential weighted rolling form (EWMA), possessions per 48/40 minutes (Basketball), Expected Goals / xG (Football), and offensive/defensive rating differentials (ORtg vs. DRtg).
- **Fatigue & Rest**: Schedule congestion, back-to-back rest penalties, and travel load.

---

## 2. Market Focus (Pure Over / Under Totals)
Selections must focus primarily on high-confidence total markets:
- **Game Over** (e.g., Over 2.5, Over 168.5)
- **Game Under** (e.g., Under 2.5, Under 171.5)
- **Team Total Over** (e.g., Team Over 1.5, Team Over 87.5)
- **Team Total Under** (e.g., Team Under 0.5, Team Under 76.5)
- **Quarter / Half Specific Totals** (1st Half Over/Under, 1st Quarter Over/Under) where market value is highest.

---

## 3. Strict Quality & Accuracy Gate
- **Prioritize statistical confidence over volume**: Never force a pick on a volatile or fifty-fifty match.
- **Required Output Format for Each Selected Pick**:
  1. **Match**: (e.g., `Paris Basketball vs ASVEL Lyon`)
  2. **Market**: (e.g., `Full Time Over 168.5 Points` or `Over 2.5 Goals`)
  3. **Confidence Level**: (e.g., `88.5% (High)` or `92.0% (Very High)`)
  4. **Projected Total**: (e.g., `178.0 Points (vs 168.5 line)`)
  5. **Brief Statistical Rationale**: Key supporting data (H2H average, recent PPG, defensive rating, pace differential).
- **Acca / Slips Suggestions**: Provide logically grouped accumulator slips (e.g., Banker Multi, High-Pace Overs, Defensive Lockdown Unders) with combined odds.
